Custom graphics FAQ

QUICK ANSWERS

How long does a custom kit take?

After you approve the proof, custom kits typically take 5–7 business days to produce before shipping.

Do I need my exact bike year and model?

Yes for full kits — plastics change by year. Include make, model, and year so we cut for the right fitment.

Can you match my colors and sponsors?

Absolutely. Describe colors and upload logos or reference photos in the quote form. We'll send a proof before printing.

What if I only need number plates or stickers?

Select that option in the form — smaller jobs often turn around faster than full kits.
